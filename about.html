Mission

Run AI more efficiently at every level.

From smarter request handling to full cost visibility, Takoraa helps companies run AI more efficiently at every level — especially as AI adoption keeps growing and infrastructure costs scale right alongside it.

We give teams the tools to understand, control, and optimize their AI spend before it becomes a problem — not after the invoice arrives.

Most teams ship AI features faster than their finance org can model the cost. By the time a CFO asks "what are we actually spending on inference?", the answer is usually "we'll get back to you." Takoraa exists to make that answer immediate, accurate, and actionable.
